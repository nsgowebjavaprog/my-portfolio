About me

I'm a final-year Computer Science student who got interested in machine learning the way most engineers do — by wanting to know what happens after the model trains. That question took me from experimenting with algorithms to building a monolingual LLM called KannadaLM from scratch, and then to shipping full MLOps pipelines with versioned data, tracked experiments, and automated deployment.

I care about the parts of ML that don't show up in a Jupyter notebook: reproducibility, CI/CD, containerization, and APIs that hold up under real traffic. My projects pair PyTorch and Scikit-learn on the modelling side with FastAPI, React, Docker, and AWS on the delivery side — because a model that isn't served reliably isn't finished.

Outside of coursework, I've picked up an AWS certification, published a research paper on AI-driven career intelligence, and competed in national and state-level hackathons. I'm currently looking for AI/ML engineering or full-stack roles where I can keep building systems end to end.
