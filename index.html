01 — About

Small models, real problems.

I'm a final-year Computer Science student who cares more about whether a model actually ships than how large it is. Most of my work sits at the seam between research and operations: training pipelines that run end to end, versioned data, tracked experiments, and deployments that survive contact with real traffic.

KannadaLM started from a gap I kept running into — almost no open language tooling exists for Kannada on its own terms. So I wrote the tokenizer, cleaned the corpus, trained the transformer, and shipped it to Hugging Face, entirely on CPU. The rest of my work — a containerized insurance-risk platform, an AWS internship, a DVC/MLflow pipeline — is the same instinct applied to more conventional ground: build the whole thing, not just the model.

04 — Work

Two builds worth a closer look.

01 Apr – May 2026

KannadaLM

The first monolingual Kannada LLM — trained, not fine-tuned.

A 7.32M-parameter transformer trained entirely from scratch on Kannada text, with a purpose-built 16K-vocabulary BPE tokenizer that never touches an English token. The full pipeline — download, clean, tokenize, train, evaluate, deploy — runs on CPU alone, at zero infrastructure cost, and ships as an open weight on Hugging Face.

  • Params7.32M, from scratch
  • Vocabulary16K BPE, Kannada-only
  • Compute100% CPU, ₹0 cost
  • Reach60M+ Kannada speakers
